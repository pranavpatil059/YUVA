HVAC, equipment health and comfort

HVAC is the biggest controllable load. Each machine is tracked against its own baseline, and every automatic action is logged so an operator can trust it or override it.

Electrical health & predictive maintenance — advanced layer

Power-quality and equipment diagnostics remain part of the BEMS: AC distribution, DC/UPS loads, harmonics, voltage unbalance and equipment health are monitored alongside energy and comfort.

Machine learning layer

Three models feed the controller. Charts are simulated model output, with a separate public BDG2 real-data validation used to demonstrate the forecasting pipeline.

Occupancy forecast

What drives tomorrow's peak-window demand

Load forecaster (LSTM)

Predicts hourly demand from weather, calendar, occupancy and tariff class.

Occupancy predictor (gradient boosting)

Learns badge, Wi-Fi and CO₂ patterns per zone to schedule HVAC and lights ahead of people.

Fault detector (isolation forest)

Scores each machine against its own healthy behaviour and names the likely cause.

Savings, payback and carbon

Change the design and see the business case move. Costs are typical Indian commercial rates, editable in the code.

What-if design

Each 1°C setpoint raise cuts HVAC energy by about 6%. Cutting HVAC in peak hours is only comfortable because the building was pre-cooled first.

Deployable architecture

Runs on existing buildings with open protocols, so a second tower is a configuration change, not a rebuild.
